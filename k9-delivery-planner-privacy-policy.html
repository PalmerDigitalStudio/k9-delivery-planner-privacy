K9 Delivery Planner Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

K9 Delivery Planner is an internal iPhone app that authorised K9 Supplies Southwest staff and drivers use to organise deliveries and depot drop-offs. This policy explains how we handle personal data about app users and people whose orders or delivery details are processed in the app.

Who is responsible for your data

K9 Supplies Southwest Ltd is the controller for the personal data we use to operate the app.

Information we use and where it comes from

  • App accounts. We use the email address and account identifier associated with your staff or driver login. Supabase Auth handles passwords, sign-in and password resets. The app stores the sign-in session in secure storage on the iPhone so you can stay signed in. The email address and sign-in credentials are needed to use the app.
  • Shopify orders. We retrieve order information from our Shopify store. Depending on the order, this can include the order number, date, value, delivery method, customer or recipient name, delivery address and postcode, contact details and order notes. These details come from information entered when an order is placed or recorded in Shopify.
  • Delivery records. Authorised app users may enter manual stops, save addresses and add or correct names, addresses and delivery notes. We store planning records such as scheduled dates, completion status and timestamps, and identifiers for the users who created or changed records.
  • Support. If you contact us about the app, we use your contact details and the information you provide to respond and investigate the issue.

Why we use it

We process customer information as necessary to fulfil orders under our contract with the customer. We rely on our legitimate interests in running and securing the delivery service for activities such as administering staff access, planning efficient routes, recording delivery outcomes, handling support and resolving disputes. That basis also covers delivery information about recipients who did not place the order themselves. Where a legal obligation requires us to keep or disclose information, we use it for that purpose.

We do not use delivery information in this app for advertising or cross-app tracking. The app suggests a route order and staff can change it. We do not use route suggestions to make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on individuals.

Who receives the information

  • Authorised staff and drivers use delivery and contact information to plan and carry out deliveries and deal with issues.
  • Shopify provides the order information from our store. Supabase provides account authentication, the database and the server functions that support the app.
  • Google Routes and Google Maps receive route start, destination and stop addresses to calculate routes or provide directions. When you open Google Maps, Google may use your device location according to your permissions and its privacy policy. K9 Delivery Planner does not collect the driver's GPS location itself.
  • Authorities or professional advisers may receive information where required by law or where necessary to deal with a legal claim.

International transfers

The app database is hosted in the UK. Some service providers may process personal data outside the UK. Where we make a restricted transfer, we rely on UK adequacy regulations where they apply or appropriate safeguards such as approved contractual terms. Contact us if you would like information about the countries and safeguards relevant to your data or a copy of those safeguards.

How long we keep information

  • Account records are needed while someone is authorised to use the app. We remove access when it is no longer needed and retain any records needed to investigate security issues or identify changes to delivery records.
  • Delivery planning records are kept while needed to complete or verify a delivery, deal with a complaint or dispute, or meet a legal obligation. Saved addresses are kept while needed for repeat deliveries.
  • Support information is kept while needed to investigate and resolve the issue and deal with any related complaint or claim.

Shopify remains the source of the original order record. Signing out removes the app session from the device. Contact us to request deletion of your account or personal data. We will explain if we need to retain any information, for example for legal obligations or claims.

Your rights and how to contact us

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask for a copy of your personal data, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict how we use it, object to processing based on legitimate interests or ask for data portability where it applies. To make a request or ask a question, email k9suppliessouthwest@gmail.com with the subject K9 Delivery Planner privacy.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office through its data protection complaints guidance.
